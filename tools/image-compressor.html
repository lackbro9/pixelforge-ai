Frequently asked questions

Does this tool upload my images anywhere?

No. Compression happens using your browser's built-in Canvas API. The image never leaves your device.

Which formats are supported?

You can upload JPG, PNG or WebP files and choose to keep the original format or convert to JPEG, WebP or PNG on output.

Why didn't the file size change much?

PNG is a lossless format, so compressing a PNG at a lower "quality" mostly affects re-encoding, not pixel data. For the biggest size reduction on photos, export as JPEG or WebP.
