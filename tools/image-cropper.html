Frequently asked questions

Can I redraw my selection?

Yes, simply click and drag again anywhere on the image to replace the current selection.

Is the crop pixel-accurate?

Yes. The tool maps your on-screen selection back to the image's real pixel dimensions before cropping, so the result matches what you selected.
