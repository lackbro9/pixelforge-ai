Frequently asked questions

Will converting JPG to PNG add transparency?

No. A JPG has no transparent areas to begin with, so the PNG result will still have a solid background — it simply uses the lossless PNG format.

Will the file get bigger?

Often yes. PNG is lossless, so photographic images usually become larger than the original JPG.
